# ClaimsPilot Motor Own-Damage Policy Wording (SYNTHETIC, SIMPLIFIED)

> Written for a portfolio prototype. It is modelled on commonly published
> motor-policy concepts but is NOT a real insurer's wording and makes no claim
> of regulatory compliance. Amounts are in Indian rupees.

## Cover
**C1 Policy period.** A loss is covered only if it happens between the policy start date and end date.

**C2 Cover type and perils.** Own-damage claims are covered only under a *comprehensive* policy,
for these perils: collision, theft, fire, flood, vandalism. Third-party-only policies do not cover damage to the insured vehicle.

## Exclusions
**E1 Driving under the influence.** No cover where the driver was under the influence of alcohol or drugs (collision claims).

**E2 No valid licence.** No cover where the driver did not hold a valid driving licence (collision claims).

**E3 Wear and tear / mechanical failure.** Loss caused by wear and tear or mechanical or electrical breakdown is excluded.

**E4 Hire, reward or racing.** No cover while the vehicle is used for hire, reward or racing.

## Notification and evidence
**N1 Notice.** The insured must report a loss within 7 days. Late notice is not a ground for refusal by itself but is reviewed (see F2).

**N2 Theft.** A theft claim needs a police report (FIR) before settlement.

## Settlement
**S1 Depreciation.** Parts are depreciated; labour is not.
- Rubber, plastic parts: 50%.  Fibreglass parts: 30%.  Glass: 0%.
- Metal parts, by vehicle age at date of loss (months): up to 6: 5%; up to 12: 15%; up to 24: 20%; up to 36: 30%; up to 48: 40%; up to 60: 50%; older: 50% (simplified).

**S2 Deductible.** The deductible on the policy schedule is subtracted from the depreciated assessed loss. If the result is zero or less, nothing is payable (D1).

**S3 Limit.** We never pay more than the insured value.

**S4 Total loss.** If the repair estimate (before depreciation) is 75% or more of the insured value, or the vehicle is stolen, the claim is settled at insured value less deductible.

## Fraud review flags (internal)
A claim with two or more of the following flags is referred for special investigation:
- **F1** Loss within 30 days of policy start.
- **F2** Reported more than 7 days after loss.
- **F3** Repair estimate at or above 70% of insured value.
- **F4** Two or more claims in the preceding 12 months.
